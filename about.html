The firm

Local firm. Institutional standard.

Parlato Real Estate is a commercial real estate brokerage based in Fishers, Indiana. We work for private owners of apartment buildings and commercial property across Indianapolis and Central Indiana.

The firm is built for owners who want the rigor of a national firm without being handed to a junior associate. Every sale assignment gets full underwriting, a professional offering memorandum and a targeted buyer campaign.
