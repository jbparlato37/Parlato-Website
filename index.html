The firm

Local firm. Institutional standard.

Parlato Real Estate is an Indianapolis-area brokerage built for owners who want the rigor of a national firm without being handed to a junior associate.

We build relationships with owners, investors, tenants and lenders across Central Indiana, and we bring every one of them to the table when it is time to transact. Every assignment gets full underwriting, a professional offering memorandum and a targeted buyer campaign.

Exclusive representation process

From first call to closing.

  1. 01

    Valuation

    We review the rent roll, operations and comps, then deliver a written opinion of value.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Pricing, timing and marketing approach set with you: on-market, off-market or both.

  3. 03

    Marketing

    Offering memorandum, targeted buyer outreach and controlled release of information.

  4. 04

    Negotiation

    Offers qualified, compared side by side and negotiated to the best price and terms.

  5. 05

    Closing

    We manage due diligence, lender and title deadlines until the deal is funded.
